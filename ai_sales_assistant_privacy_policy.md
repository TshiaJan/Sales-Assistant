# Privacy Policy for AI Sales Assistant

**Effective Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026  

Welcome to **AI Sales Assistant** ("the Application"), developed and maintained by **Tshiamo Jantjie** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of sales professionals, account executives, business development representatives, and revenue managers who use our Application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Application, emphasizing our local-first architecture and commitment to user data privacy.

---

### 1. Architectural Summary & Local-First Processing

AI Sales Assistant is designed as an autonomous revenue acceleration and sales enablement application. All operational sales data—including pipeline opportunities, deal stages, estimated contract values, closing probabilities, playbook notes, email drafts, objection handling prompts, team leaderboards, and revenue projections—is processed and stored locally on your device using client-side storage mechanisms (such as  and ).

We do not operate central cloud databases that ingest, harvest, or commercialize your confidential deals, customer names, or sales strategies.

---

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect

To ensure strict enterprise and personal confidentiality:
- **No Mandatory Account Registration:** You can access and utilize all core sales intelligence features without creating an account, linking social identities (Google, Apple, Microsoft), or submitting your phone number or corporate email.
- **No Direct Financial or Banking Credentials:** The revenue calculations, ARR/MRR simulations, pricing plan builders, and affiliate models provided within the app are strictly computational simulations. We never request, capture, or store credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment processing tokens.
- **No Customer Contact Harvesting:** The app does not scrape your device address book, email inbox, or mobile contacts. Any prospect or client details entered into mock deal cards remain exclusively in local device storage.
- **No Sale or Brokerage of Private Data:** We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your sales records, team metrics, or generated objection playbooks to third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or competing platforms.

---

### 3. Optional Diagnostics and Non-Personal Telemetry

To ensure technical reliability and compatibility across Android devices, the Application may utilize standard Google Play platform services for anonymous technical diagnostics:
- **Crash Logs & Performance Metrics:** Anonymous stack traces and device model information to diagnose application crashes, memory leaks, and render issues.
- **Aggregated Installation Statistics:** Non-identifiable metrics provided by the Google Play Console (such as device OS version, display resolution, and country of download).
- **Advertising / Diagnostic Identifiers:** If optional contextual ad networks or Google Play Services analytics are enabled, non-personal pseudonymous device identifiers may be processed in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

---

### 4. User Control, Data Portability, and Complete Erasure

Because your data lives directly in your device's browser/application storage:
- **Data Erasure:** You can permanently purge all stored deals, pipelines, and customized playbooks at any time by clearing the Application's storage and cache via your Android system settings (), or by uninstalling the Application.
- **No Residual Cloud Backups:** Once cleared on your physical device, no persistent copies remain on remote servers under our control.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services and External Links

The Application may display links or references to external resources, documentation, or optional web services. If you navigate to external third-party websites or services, their independent privacy policies govern your interactions. We encourage you to review their privacy statements before providing personal information.

---

### 6. Children's Online Privacy Protection

AI Sales Assistant is designed specifically for enterprise sales professionals, corporate teams, and business operators. The Application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
